## Phase Goal

**As a** chess GUI or UCI test harness driving ANCE over stdin/stdout, **I want to** complete the handshake and receive exactly one legal `bestmove` for every `go` — even through a swappable evaluator — **so that** I can play a complete, legal game and confirm ANCE reliably beats a random-mover opponent.

<objective>
Build the self-play harness that pits ANCE's real search+eval (in-process,
not over a UCI pipe — this is a measurement tool, not a protocol test)
against a uniformly-random legal-move opponent, and prove ANCE reliably
beats it.

Purpose: TOOL-02 is one of the milestone's two hard proof points (with
TOOL-01). A correct engine with a full evaluator should never lose to a
purely random opponent.

REPLAN (approved 2026-07-07, during execution of this plan): the original
acceptance criterion — wins == 100, losses == 0 out of 100 games at
GAUNTLET_SEARCH_DEPTH = 4 — proved both impractical and unverified. A real
depth-4/100-game run was killed after 31 minutes without finishing
(unpruned pure-Python negamax is too slow at depth 4 for a per-wave test),
and a depth-3 spot check already drew, so even the strict 100/0 target was
never actually measured green. The invariant that DOES hold, and was
measured deterministically (seeds 0..29, depth 2, ~31s wall-clock): ANCE
NEVER loses to the random mover (25 wins / 0 losses / 5 draws, 83% win
rate), and all 5 draws are `max_halfmoves`(300) cap conversions — shallow
search finds the winning edge but can't force mate within the cap — never
a loss or stalemate.

The new, authoritative acceptance criterion: `losses == 0` is a HARD
invariant (a loss to a uniformly-random mover is always a real bug); the
strength floor is `wins >= 70%` of games, with every non-win required to
be a draw. The slow test runs `n_games=30` at `GAUNTLET_SEARCH_DEPTH = 2`
(fast, ~31s, deterministic 25/0/5 at authoring). The original "100 wins /
0 draws at depth 4" goal is DEFERRED — tracked as a follow-up — until
alpha-beta pruning (a later search phase per ROADMAP.md) makes deeper
search practical in wall-clock time; it is not asserted by this plan.

Output: `ance/tools/random_mover_gauntlet.py` plus a `slow`-marked pytest
that asserts `losses == 0`, `wins >= 70%`, and every non-win is a draw,
with built-in failure diagnostics.
</objective>

    machine:

       (seed, result, terminal FEN) to understand what actually happened
       before changing anything — most non-wins are diagnosable from
       these three fields alone (e.g. a halfmove-cap draw vs. an actual
       tactical blunder or an actual loss).

       depth as an ad hoc fix without pruning.

       `ance/search/negamax.py` to fix a gauntlet failure: it is
       deliberately decoupled from `GAUNTLET_SEARCH_DEPTH` precisely so
       GUI-responsiveness tuning never weakens the gauntlet's proof, and
       adjusting it here would not address a gauntlet-strength issue
       anyway.

       outside the full 30-game run for focused debugging.
