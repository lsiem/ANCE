## Phase Goal

**As a** chess GUI or UCI test harness driving ANCE over stdin/stdout, **I want to** complete the handshake and receive exactly one legal `bestmove` for every `go` — even through a swappable evaluator — **so that** I can play a complete, legal game and confirm ANCE reliably beats a random-mover opponent.

to build on top of it.

that a GUI can drive with confidence the board never silently corrupts.

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| stdin `position fen ...` / `moves ...` | Fully untrusted strings — a GUI, arbiter, or adversarial pipe script can send any bytes here |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-03 | Denial of Service | ance/board/position.py try_set_fen/try_push_uci_moves | high | mitigate | Every parse of untrusted FEN/move strings goes through a single `try/except ValueError` boundary (covers `chess.InvalidMoveError`/`chess.IllegalMoveError`) that never lets an exception propagate to the dispatcher; candidate board built locally, swapped in only on success |
| T-01-04 | Tampering | ance/board/position.py partial move-list application | medium | mitigate | `try_push_uci_moves` operates on a full local copy and only commits after every move in the list succeeds — a partially-applied move list can never leak into `self.board` |
| T-01-05 | Information Disclosure | ance/debug.py | low | accept | Debug output only reaches local stderr (never stdout, never a network sink) and is off by default; acceptable for a local diagnostic channel |

</threat_model>
