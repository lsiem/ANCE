## Phase Goal

**As a** chess player running ANCE under a real clock in a GUI, **I want to** play blitz games against an engine that searches efficiently and manages its own time, **so that** games are decided by chess strength rather than shuffling, shallow search, or time forfeits.

<objective>
Build the reusable self-play gauntlet harness (TOOL-03): a python-chess arbiter that referees clocks itself (default runner on this machine — cutechess-cli has no macOS binary, verified in RESEARCH), plays two engine commands from a fixed opening book with color parity, checkpoints per game, resumes after interruption, and reports W-L-D + draw rate + Wilson 95% CI. Honors D-15 through D-19.

Purpose: TOOL-03 is the measurement backbone for D-14's zero-forfeit gate (Plan 03-06), D-17's 50% sanity check, and Phase 5's NNUE-vs-handcrafted Elo gauntlet.
Output: `ance/tools/gauntlet.py`, `ance/tools/openings.epd`, and fast contract tests.
</objective>
