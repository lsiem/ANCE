## Phase Goal

**As a** chess player running ANCE under a real clock in a GUI, **I want to** play blitz games against an engine that searches efficiently and manages its own time, **so that** games are decided by chess strength rather than shuffling, shallow search, or time forfeits.

<objective>
Snapshot the Phase 2 search baseline (D-20) BEFORE any transposition-table or move-ordering code lands: completed depth and cumulative nodes at a fixed 2s movetime budget, plus deterministic nodes-to-fixed-depth, on a fixed FEN set. This artifact is the honest yardstick D-21's "measurably better" claim is judged against in Plan 03-04.

Purpose: SRCH-06's success criterion says ordering must "measurably increase cutoffs / reach greater depth than the Phase 2 baseline at equal time" — without a pre-change snapshot there is nothing to measure against, and the snapshot is unrecoverable once negamax.py changes.
Output: `ance/tools/phase3_baseline.py` collector CLI, fast contract tests, and the committed `.planning/phases/03-search-acceleration-time-management/03-BASELINE.json` artifact.
</objective>
