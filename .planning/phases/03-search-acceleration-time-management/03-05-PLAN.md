## Phase Goal

**As a** chess player running ANCE under a real clock in a GUI, **I want to** play blitz games against an engine that searches efficiently and manages its own time, **so that** games are decided by chess strength rather than shuffling, shallow search, or time forfeits.

<objective>
Implement SRCH-08/UCI-08: act on the already-parsed wtime/btime/winc/binc fields (D-11) with a soft/hard budget (D-12), preserve the movetime/depth/infinite precedence (D-13), gate iterative-deepening iterations on the soft budget, and shrink the hard-stop poll interval so the 200ms safety margin is real at 10-50k nps. After this plan a user can play a clocked blitz game against ANCE in En Croissant and the engine budgets its own time.

Purpose: never losing on time (D-14) is a phase acceptance gate; this plan makes the engine clock-aware, Plan 03-06 proves it over 100 games.
Output: `ance/uci/clock.py`, handle_go clock branch, soft-limit gate in search_root, poll fix, and flag-safety tests.
</objective>
